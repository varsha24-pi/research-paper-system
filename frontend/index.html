System Architecture & Capabilities

Designed for academic rigor, transparent explainability, and high-performance retrieval.

PDF Extraction Engine

🧹

NLP Section Segmentation

Automatically detects and segments papers into standard sections: Abstract, Introduction, Methodology, Results, and Conclusion.
